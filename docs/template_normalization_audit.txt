AI CUSTOMER SUPPORT INTELLIGENCE
TEMPLATE NORMALIZATION AUDIT
========================================

Objective:
Investigate repeated ticket descriptions
containing template-generated placeholders.

Normalization:
The placeholder {product_purchased}
was replaced with PLACEHOLDER for
comparison of normalized descriptions.

Findings:
- Multiple records share similar normalized descriptions.
- The same template appears with different Ticket Type labels.
- Examples include Technical issue,
  Cancellation request, Product inquiry,
  and Billing inquiry.

Conclusion:
Template-generated descriptions may
contain insufficient information for
reliable ticket classification.

Further investigation is required before
production model training.
